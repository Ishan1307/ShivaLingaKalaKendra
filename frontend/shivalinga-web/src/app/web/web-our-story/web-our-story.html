Where a love of dance
found a home.

The idea for Shivalinga Kala Kendra began with a simple feeling: dance can hold our stories, connect us to our roots, and give us the courage to become more fully ourselves. That feeling became a place where movement could be learned with care and shared with joy.

Our founder imagined an academy where discipline and creativity belong together, where tradition is honoured without standing still, and where every student is welcomed as they are. That intention continues to guide each class, each rehearsal, and every time we step onto the stage.

This is more than a place to learn steps. It is a community built on patience, expression, and the belief that the arts can help us grow — together.

A note from our founder

“
I began Shivalinga Kala Kendra with a belief that dance is more than learning steps. It is a way to honour where we come from and discover who we can become.

My hope is that every student who walks through our doors finds discipline, joy, confidence, and a place to belong. May we keep learning, creating, and carrying this beautiful art forward — together.

With gratitude, Arohi Ishan Pandey
